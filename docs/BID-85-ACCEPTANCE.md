## Device acceptance gates

Parked vehicle only. Confirm default HOME, repeat ACC off/on after short and long standby; distinguish reboot and process recreation. Compare OEM CAN screen with BID samples; confirm source, units, freshness and unavailable values. Never activate safety warnings from unauthenticated broadcasts. Verify original 23 touch zones, installed-app picker, editor persistence, backups, native widgets, map authorization, and rollback. Record exact APK SHA and signing identity. Build success alone is not device acceptance.
